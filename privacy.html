Application: Shri Hanuman Chalisa (श्री हनुमान चालीसा)

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Shri Hanuman Chalisa ("we", "our", or "the application") manages information when you use our mobile application on iOS and iPadOS devices. We are committed to preserving your privacy and providing a serene, transparent, and spiritually uplifting devotional experience.

Summary: We do not require registration or personal account creation. We do not collect or sell your personal information (such as your name, email, address, or contacts). Local preferences (favorites, japa counts, puzzle scores) remain strictly on your device.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

When you download and use Shri Hanuman Chalisa, we do not require you to provide, nor do we collect:

  • Personal Identifiers (such as your name, physical address, or phone number).
  • Contact lists, address book entries, or social media profiles.
  • Precise or real-time GPS location data.
  • Biometric, microphone, or camera data.
  • Financial, credit card, or bank account information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The application stores certain user preferences locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device and includes:

  • Bookmarked or favorite verses and stutis.
  • Digital Japa Mala repetition counts and completion milestones.
  • Devotional daily puzzle progress, completed state, and move counts.
  • Audio playback configurations (e.g., chanting tempo, auto-advance preferences).

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To support ongoing development and maintenance while keeping the application accessible to all devotees, we integrate reputable third-party services that may collect diagnostic or advertising data:

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to deliver advertisements (banners on the puzzle screen, optional rewarded videos, and frequency-capped interstitials) to non-subscribed users. Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (such as Apple IDFA, IDFV, or Android Advertising ID, where authorized).
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location and fraud prevention).
  • App performance data, crash logs, and ad interaction metrics.

You can learn more about how Google processes information by reviewing Google's Advertising Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy & Terms.

B. Apple In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

We offer an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription (adsfree) that removes all advertisements and provides uninterrupted devotional reading and puzzle auto-solve. All purchase transactions are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. through the App Store and StoreKit APIs:

  • Apple manages your payment details, billing address, and transaction authentication securely via your Apple ID.
  • The developer does not have access to your payment card details, billing credentials, or financial accounts.
  • Subscription status is verified securely on-device via Apple StoreKit receipts.

4. User Choices & Managing Ad Preferences

You have full control over your privacy and advertising choices:

  • Ad-Free Devotional Subscription: You can completely remove all banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads by subscribing to the ad-free plan within the app.
  • Managing Subscriptions: Subscriptions can be viewed, managed, or cancelled at any time by visiting your Apple ID Account Settings -> Subscriptions in your device's Settings app.
  • Apple App Tracking Transparency: You can choose whether to allow advertising tracking via iOS Settings -> Privacy & Security -> Tracking.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Shri Hanuman Chalisa is designed as a family-friendly devotional application suitable for general audiences of all ages (rated 4+). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

6. Data Security

Because the app does not maintain central user databases or collect personal credentials, your devotional data (such as bookmarks and chanting progress) remains secure on your personal device and is governed by your device's security and Apple iOS sandboxing safeguards.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, App Store requirements, or applicable legal standards. Any modifications will be posted with an updated effective date at the top of this page.
